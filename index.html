    About

    Hey, I'm Fateh.

    I design brands, interfaces, motion and books, and I like to bring whatever I make to life with motion.

    I'm a visual communication designer from Lahore. I studied at Beaconhouse National University.

    Outside design, I'm on a football pitch or deep inside a video game. I read crime cases the way other people read novels. Nothing makes sense at first, then one small clue changes everything.

    Games taught me how a story moves. A clue, a twist, then the moment it all clicks. I design the same way. Every brand, screen and book is a small case to solve, and I want the person on the other side to feel that click.
